# Hi, I'm Bastien Detemmerman 

Physical Oceanographer | Hydrodynamic Modeler | Data Enthusiast

I am an oceanographer with a Master's degree in Marine Physics (PHYMER) and a highly atypical, operational background. Before diving into fluid dynamics and coastal modeling, I spent 15 years in fast-food management. This unique path gave me an undeniable edge in crisis management, high-stress decision making, and team resilience—skills I now apply to operational oceanography and water quality forecasting.

### What I do
- Hydrodynamic Modeling: Solving complex physical problems (Navier-Stokes, baroclinic modes) and configuring coastal models (CROCO).
- Data Engineering: Extracting, processing, and interpolating massive multi-source oceanographic datasets (Copernicus NetCDF, MARC, ADCP, CTD).
- Machine Learning & Spatial Analysis: Applying statistical learning (XGBoost) and bio-optical inversions (Lee model) to monitor coastal environments.
- Fluid Dynamics Simulation: Experimenting with modern Python CFD frameworks (like Phiflow) for real-time advection and Navier-Stokes projection.
